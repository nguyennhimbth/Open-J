# JEOPARDY! OFFICIAL GAME RULES (SEASON 40 & 41 STANDARD)

## 1. Match Overview
Each game features **3 contestants** competing across **3 main rounds**:
- **Round 1:** Jeopardy! Round
- **Round 2:** Double Jeopardy! Round
- **Round 3:** Final Jeopardy!

All valid responses must adhere to the classic grammatical convention: **Phrased in the form of a question** (e.g., *"Who is...?"* or *"What is...?"*).

---

## 2. Round 1: Jeopardy! Round

### Board Configuration
- Grid of **6 categories** $\times$ **5 clue tiers**.
- Fixed point values: **200, 400, 600, 800, and 1,000 points**.

### Buzzer Mechanism (Lockout System)
- The host reads the clue entirely.
- Enabling lights flanking the board illuminate to open the buzzers:
  - The first contestant to trigger the signal at the millisecond level earns the attempt.
  - Buzzing in prematurely (*jumping the gun*) incurs an automatic **0.25-second lockout penalty**.
- Response countdown: **5 seconds** from the moment the player is recognized.

### Scoring
- **Correct:** Adds the clue's point value to the contestant's score. The player gains control of the board.
- **Incorrect / Out of Time:** Deducts the full clue value from the player's score. The remaining two contestants may buzz in for a rebound attempt (one attempt per player).

### Special Tile: Daily Double
- Exactly **1 Daily Double** is hidden randomly on the board.
- Exclusive attempt: Only the contestant who reveals it may respond (buzzers are disabled).
- **Wagering Rules:**
  - Minimum wager: **5 points**.
  - If current score $\le 1,000$: Maximum wager is **1,000 points**.
  - If current score $> 1,000$: Maximum wager equals the player's **entire current score** (*True Daily Double*).

---

## 3. Round 2: Double Jeopardy! Round

### Board Configuration
- Grid of **6 brand-new categories** $\times$ **5 clue tiers**.
- Doubled point values: **400, 800, 1,200, 1,600, and 2,000 points**.
- The contestant with the lowest score at the end of Round 1 chooses the first clue.

### Special Tiles: Daily Doubles
- Exactly **2 Daily Doubles** are hidden randomly on the board.
- **Wagering Rules:**
  - Minimum wager: **5 points**.
  - If current score $\le 2,000$: Maximum wager is **2,000 points**.
  - If current score $> 2,000$: Maximum wager equals the player's **entire current score**.

---

## 4. Round 3: Final Jeopardy!

### Eligibility
- Only contestants with a **positive score ($> 0$)** at the end of Double Jeopardy! qualify.
- Players with $\le 0$ points are eliminated from the game prior to this round.

### Execution Procedure
1. **Category Reveal:** The host announces the category.
2. **Blind Wager:** Contestants have a commercial break interval to secretly input their wagers:
   - Minimum wager: **0 points**.
   - Maximum wager: The player's entire available score.
3. **Clue & 30-Second Countdown:** The host reads the clue, triggering the **30-second music timer**.
4. **Submission:** Contestants write their response (in question format) on their personal console before the 30-second timer expires.

---

## 5. Winner Determination & Consolation Prizes

### Final Scoring
- Correct Final Jeopardy response: **Adds** the wagered points.
- Incorrect / Blank Final Jeopardy response: **Deducts** the wagered points.
- The contestant with the highest overall score wins the match.

### Prize Structure (Season 40 & 41 Standard)
| Rank | Award / Cash Equivalent |
|---|---|
| **1st Place (Winner)** | Retains their total cumulative score as earnings and advances to the next episode |
| **2nd Place (Runner-up)** | Fixed award: **3,000 points** (or $3,000) |
| **3rd Place** | Fixed award: **2,000 points** (or $2,000) |

*(Note: 2nd and 3rd place finishers receive standard fixed consolation payouts regardless of their final on-screen board total).*
